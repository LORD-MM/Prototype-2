# Prototype 2: Feed the Animals

A simple top-down 3D game from Unity's **Junior Programmer** pathway (Create with Code). Animals run toward you, and you move left and right throwing food at them before they get past.

- **Controls:** move left and right, press the fire key to throw food, press the spawn key to send a random animal
- Food and animals are removed once they leave the play area

## Details
- **Unity version:** Unity 6.6 (6000.6.0f1)
- **Input:** New Input System
- **Scene:** `Assets/Scenes/Prototype 2.unity`
